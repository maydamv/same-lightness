Same lightness?

Two colours with the same HSL lightness don't look equally light. Why?

1 · Same L, different light

Every colour in a row has the same lightness number. The grey under each one is how much light it really sends to your eye.

      Click a colour to see its maths below.

      2 · Under the hood

      HSL's lightness is only the middle point between the strongest and the weakest channel. It doesn't know that our eyes are much more sensitive to green than to blue. Relative luminance does.
