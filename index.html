      Under the hood

      HSL's lightness is only the middle point between the strongest and the weakest channel. It doesn't know that our eyes are much more sensitive to green than to blue. Relative luminance does.

      Can you read it?

      Text needs enough difference in light from its background. WCAG measures it with the same luminance: contrast = (lighter + 0.05) / (darker + 0.05).

        Not everyone sees the same colours

        About 1 in 12 men and 1 in 200 women see colours differently. Pick a type: every colour on this page changes to how they would see it. The luminance numbers don't change, because the light is the same; only the hue gets lost.
